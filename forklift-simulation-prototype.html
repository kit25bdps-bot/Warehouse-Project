Scenario Briefing

Deliver the Pallet

A loaded pallet is sitting in the yellow zone. Your job is to get it safely into the green bay. Nothing on screen will tell you what to do next — you're expected to know the routine and run it yourself.

  • Nothing moves until the engine is running.
  • The forks have to be down and lined up before a load will attach.
  • Driving with a raised load is marked as unsafe.
  • Releasing outside the bay drops the load — you'll need to recover it.
